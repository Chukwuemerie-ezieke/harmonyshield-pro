Frequently asked questions

Who is HavonaShield for?

Primary, secondary and tertiary institutions in Nigeria, and groups or ministries that oversee several schools.

Where is our data stored?

HavonaShield runs on Cloudflare's network with an encrypted managed PostgreSQL database. Each school's data is logically isolated and only visible to that school's users.

Does HavonaShield make us compliant automatically?

No tool can do that on its own. HavonaShield organises the work, tracks progress and keeps evidence, so your DPO and leadership can demonstrate compliance with confidence.

Can we try it first?

Yes. The live demo is a read-only sample school you can explore without signing up.
